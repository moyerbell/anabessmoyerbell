About

Theatre, therapy &
community practice.

Ana Bess Moyer Bell is a drama therapist, playwright, educator, and harm reduction advocate. Her work is grounded in a commitment to dignity and unconditional support for people who use drugs.

She co-created Creative Pathways at the Genesis Center in Providence and teaches in the Expressive Therapies Graduate Program at Lesley University. She holds an M.A. in Drama Therapy from New York University.

Selected work.

Creative Pathways

Co-created in 2024 at the Genesis Center in Providence, Creative Pathways brings together English language learning, creative writing, visual arts, and drama therapy for newly arrived Rhode Islanders. Its “Recipes for Belonging” theme celebrates food, memory, and community connection.

Theatre & recovery

Ana Bess founded COAAST in 2016 and later helped bring COAAST and Improbable Players together as 2nd Act. Her work has included therapeutic theatre, education for healthcare professionals, and public health films that challenge stigma around substance use.

Harm reduction & advocacy

Working alongside people with lived experience, public health leaders, and elected officials, Ana Bess has advocated for drug policy reform. Her work in Rhode Island has included support for overdose prevention sites, naloxone access, and the peer recovery workforce.

Clinical practice & teaching

Her clinical experience includes work with trauma, grief, substance use, and chronic illness. She brings that experience to community programs and her teaching in the Expressive Therapies Graduate Program at Lesley University.
